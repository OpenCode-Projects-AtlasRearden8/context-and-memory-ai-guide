# The Context Audit Worksheet

---

Fill this out for real — on paper, in your notes app, or under each question in a chat. It takes about 15 minutes and it's the highest-leverage quarter hour you'll spend on your AI habits. Be honest; nobody else sees it.

## Part 1: Your current chats

**1. Count them.** How many ongoing chats do you have *right now*? ____

**2. The mega-thread test.** Look at the chat you use most. How many *different* topics does it cover?

- 1 → excellent
- 2-3 → normal, sub-optimal surroundings
- 4+ → you have a mega-thread. Chapter 4 is calling TO you.

**3. The "as I said" test.** In the last week, how many times did you type something like "as I mentioned earlier…" or "remember when I said…"? ____

Each one is a signal the chat lost the thread before YOU did.

## Part 2: Your context habits

**4. The header test.** Do you have a saved context header (the Chapter 5 template) you can paste into a fresh chat?

- [ ] Yes, and I use it
- [ ] I made one once but don't use it
- [ ] No — but I'll make it tonight

**5. The handoff test.** When a good chat ends, do you get/download/save a handoff note (the Chapter 4 closing ritual)?

- [ ] Yes, every significant chat
- [ ] Sometimes, if I remember
- [ ] Never — my context lives in browser tabs and vibes

**6. The paste test.** When you paste a document, what do you usually include?

- [ ] Just the relevant section, with an on-ramp message (Chapter 6 style)
- [ ] The whole document and hope for the best
- [ ] All of my documents, all of the time (starts a small fire every time)

**7. The re-anchor test.** When answers drift, what do you currently do?

- [ ] Re-anchor / restate instruction beside the task (Chapter 7 steps 1-2)
- [ ] Get a recap and correct it (Chapter 7 step 3)
- [ ] Start a fresh chat with context pasted in (Chapter 7 step 4)
- [ ] Sigh, delete, re-explain, and blame the model

## Part 3: Your memory settings

**8. Have you looked at your main tool's saved-memory list in the past month?**

- [ ] Yes
- [ ] No
- [ ] I didn't know that existed

If "no" to either of the latter two: schedule the Chapter 8 audit right now. It takes 5 minutes.

**9. Are your identity-level prefs (custom instructions) current?**

- [ ] Yes, written deliberately
- [ ] They were set once by past-me and I've never re-read them (past-me thanks you for nothing)
- [ ] No idea where they are

**10. What stays in your chats?** In the last 7 chats, whenever you pasted a document, did you strip personal identifiers where you could?

- [ ] Mostly yes
- [ ] Not really
- [ ] I don't think about that (today you do — see Chapter 6)

## Part 4: The one thing

**11. If you could fix exactly ONE habit this week, which would have the biggest effect?**

____

*My pick for most people, if you want a suggestion: build and save the context header (question 4). Everything else gets easier after that.*

## The follow-through

Pick your one fix, then write the sentence: "This week, I will ____. Then I will know it worked because ____."

Keep this worksheet. Re-do it in a month. The second time through feels like a different person wrote the first one.
